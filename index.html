01 — ABOUT ME

A foundation in Civil Engineering, built step by step

My journey in Civil Engineering began at Chattogram Polytechnic Institute, where I completed a four-year Diploma in Civil Technology (2021–2025) with a CGPA of 3.81 out of 4.00. The diploma gave me a practical grounding in construction materials, building planning and technical drawing, and it was there that I learned to prepare architectural and residential plans in AutoCAD.

After the diploma, I spent three months as a site engineering intern, where I saw how drawings, materials and people come together on an active construction site. That experience showed me how much there still is to learn, and it strengthened my wish to understand engineering more deeply.

In 2026 I began my B.Sc. in Civil Engineering at Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET), where I am currently in my first semester. I enjoy technical problem-solving and careful, well-documented work, and I am committed to learning continuously. In the long term, I hope to pursue higher studies and build a career in academia as a university lecturer and researcher.
